      ABOUT

      How Friend Zone Bus Works

      Friend Zone Bus is an interpretive tool designed to help you think about complicated relationship signals.

      It looks at two different things: signals and what has actually been communicated.

      Someone can flirt without wanting a relationship. Someone can talk about another person without sending a romantic message. And someone can behave like a close friend without ever having rejected romantic interest.

      That's why Friend Zone Bus doesn't simply ask:

      "Do they like you?"

      It asks:

      "Where does the evidence actually take you?"
      REALITY CHECK

      Disclaimer

      Friend Zone Bus is an interpretive tool designed for personal reflection and entertainment.

      It cannot read another person's mind, determine their private feelings, or guarantee what they want from a relationship.

      Your result is based entirely on the information you provide. A romantic signal may have several possible explanations, and a platonic signal does not necessarily mean someone has rejected you romantically.

      The assessment should therefore not be treated as proof of another person's feelings, intentions, or relationship status.

      If you want to know how someone actually feels, honest and respectful communication with that person is ultimately more reliable than any assessment.

      We're mapping the evidence you have — not claiming to know what's inside someone else's head.
